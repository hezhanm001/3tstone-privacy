Welcome to "Market Sniper" (the "App"), developed and operated by 3TStone ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information and is designed to comply with the Apple App Store and Google Play Store developer policies, as well as major global privacy frameworks including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), China's Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI), South Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), and Australia's Privacy Act 1988.

Because this App is designed as a 100% offline, standalone game, our approach to privacy is simple: we collect nothing.

We provide this Policy in multiple languages. If there is any inconsistency between language versions, the English version prevails unless applicable law requires otherwise.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, store, process, transmit, or share any Personal Identifiable Information (PII) or personal data of any kind. This includes, but is not limited to, your real name, email address, physical address, phone number, payment details, device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP address, or precise or approximate location.

Local-Only Storage

Any data generated during your gameplay — including the User ID you choose to enter, your selected game duration (1, 3, 5, or 10 years), your in-game investment decisions, and your virtual portfolio — is stored exclusively on your local device. This data never leaves your device. The User ID you enter is a locally-stored, user-defined string that is not linked to any real-world identity and is never transmitted off your device.

No Network Transmission

The App does not connect to any remote servers, cloud databases, or third-party APIs at any time. All historical market data, anonymous stock mappings, and historical news content are pre-packaged locally within the App at the time of installation.

2. Device Permissions

The App requires no sensitive device permissions. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, precise or approximate location, or any other sensitive system resource. The App only utilizes standard local app storage to read and save your game progress on your device.

3. Third-Party Services and Tracking

No Third-Party SDKs

We do not integrate any third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, social media plug-ins, crash reporting services, or tracking technologies of any kind.

No Data Sharing

Because we collect no data, we have nothing to sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer to any third party.

4. Compliance with Global Privacy Regulations

Because the App operates entirely offline and collects zero personal data, it inherently satisfies the core obligations of all major global privacy frameworks. The following is provided for transparency:

GDPR (European Union) & UK GDPR: We do not process any personal data of EU or UK users. As all data resides solely on your device, you can exercise your right to erasure at any time simply by clearing the App's local data through your device's system settings, or by uninstalling the App.

CCPA/CPRA (California, USA): We do not "sell" or "share" consumer personal information as defined under the CCPA or CPRA.

COPPA (USA): We do not knowingly collect any personal information from any person, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for general audiences.

PIPL (China): We do not collect, use, process, or transfer any personal information of users within mainland China.

PDPA (Singapore), APPI (Japan), PIPA (South Korea), LGPD (Brazil), Privacy Act 1988 (Australia): We do not collect or process any personal data of users in these jurisdictions.

5. Data Security

Although we do not collect any data, we are committed to ensuring the integrity of the App itself. All game content and historical data are securely packaged within the App binary and are not modifiable by external parties.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable law. Any updates will be reflected by a new "Last Updated" date at the top of this Policy and will be made available within the App or via app store update release notes. Your continued use of the App after any such changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.
