1. Introduction

I/E Diary (hereinafter referred to as "the App") is provided by 3TStone Ltd (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use the App.

The App is currently not available on app stores in mainland China, and we have excluded this region in the distribution settings of the Apple App Store and Google Play. If you are located in mainland China, please do not download or use the App.

This policy may be provided in multiple languages. In the event of any ambiguity or conflict between the translated versions, the English version shall prevail.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

The email address you provide during registration, along with your securely encrypted account credentials.

2.2 Financial Records

The income and expense entries you voluntarily input, including transaction amount, currency, category, notes, and transaction date.

2.3 Media (Subscribers Only)

Photos (e.g., receipt or invoice images) that you voluntarily attach to your transaction records.

[Important Note: 3-Year Retention Period for Photos]
Photos are securely stored in the cloud for exactly 3 years from the date of upload. Upon the expiration of the 3-year period, the system will automatically and permanently delete the photo, even if your subscription remains active. Please ensure you back up important receipt or invoice images properly.
This mandatory 3-year deletion mechanism applies only to uploaded photos. Text-based transaction records (such as amounts, dates, and categories) are not affected and will be retained until you actively delete the record or your account.

2.4 Demographic Data (For Users Who Enable Data Sharing)

If you actively choose to enable the data sharing feature (see Section 3.2), we will collect the following optional information for group comparison purposes: age group, country or region, state/province, industry, family status, family size, and personal annual income range. All the above information is optional and can be updated or deleted within the App at any time.

2.5 Customer Support and Feedback Data

When you contact us using the in-app feedback feature, we collect the feedback subject, text content, attached screenshots, as well as your account email address and User ID. This information is used solely to resolve your issues, improve App quality, and provide customer support.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Core Features

We use your information to provide core bookkeeping functionalities, synchronize your transaction history across multiple devices via the cloud, verify account identity, and comply with relevant legal obligations.

3.2 Data Sharing and Group Comparison Feature

The App offers an optional data sharing feature that allows you to contribute your transaction data to group statistics for comparison against groups with specific criteria (e.g., same age group, same industry).

  • When you turn it on: The transaction data you input thereafter (limited to amount, date, and category) and the demographic information you provided will be used for group comparisons.
  • When you turn it off and withdraw historical data: Once you turn off the sharing toggle, any newly inputted transaction data will no longer be used for group comparisons. Additionally, you can permanently delete any historical transaction data you contributed to the comparison pool using the dedicated button in the App. This withdrawal and deletion operation will not affect your personal transaction records stored in the cloud for your own viewing.
  • Privacy Protection Mechanism (Pseudonymization and Aggregation): Shared data undergoes "Pseudonymization" processing in the background. This pseudonymized data remains strictly protected under data protection laws (such as the UK GDPR), and you retain full data subject rights over it (see Section 6). When comparison results are displayed to other users, the data is strictly aggregated, meaning no one can view the specific records of a single user.
  • Data That is NEVER Shared: Under no circumstances will your transaction photos, transaction notes, or account credentials be uploaded to the comparison database or shared with other users. They are kept strictly confidential.

3.3 Automated Decision-Making

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Data Retention Periods

In accordance with the principle of data minimization, we retain your personal information only as long as necessary:

Data Type Retention Period
Account & text-based transaction records Retained indefinitely until you manually delete individual records or your entire account.
Uploaded photos Stored securely for exactly 3 years from the upload date, then automatically permanently deleted.
Pseudonymized data in comparison database Retained indefinitely until you: 1) withdraw historical shared data via the App; 2) manually delete the corresponding personal transaction record; or 3) delete your entire account.
Customer support and feedback data Retained for a maximum of 2 years after your issue is resolved, then automatically deleted.
Legal / Compliance data Retained for up to 7 years where required by law (e.g., tax records).

If we cease operations, we will immediately stop all personal information collection activities, notify you via in-app notification or email, and delete or anonymize all personal information we hold.

6. Your Rights

Regarding the personal data (including pseudonymized data) we process, you possess the following core rights:

  • Right to Access — To obtain confirmation of whether we process your data and a copy of the data.
  • Right to Rectification — To correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure (Deletion) — To request the deletion of your data.
  • Right to Restrict Processing — To request that we limit how your data is used.
  • Right to Data Portability — To receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Right to Object — To object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — To withdraw your consent for the data sharing feature at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

Users in specific regions may also enjoy other rights granted by their local mandatory laws, including but not limited to the California (CCPA/CPRA) right to opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (we do not sell personal information); South Korea (PIPA) right to temporary suspension; Japan (APPI) right to cease utilization; Taiwan right to cease collection; and Hong Kong (PDPO), Macau (PDPA), Singapore, and Malaysia (PDPA) rights to access and correction.

How to Exercise Your Rights:
You can exercise some rights directly within the App's "Settings" page:

  • "Anonymous Sample Comparison" toggle: Controls future data sharing.
  • Clear historical shared data button: Withdraws and deletes your historical pseudonymized data from the comparison database with one click.
  • "Security Settings > Delete Account": Permanently deletes your account and all associated data.

Please note that some data (such as original account registration credentials) cannot be directly modified or deleted through the app interface. If needed, please contact us via email to handle this.

To request to exercise other rights (including data access, export, or restriction), please email support@3tstone.com. We may verify your identity before replying and will respond within the following timeframes: 1 month under UK GDPR; 15 business days under CCPA; 30 days for other general inquiries.
