Last Updated: June 29, 2026

Contact: support@3tstone.com

This Privacy Policy explains how 3TStone Ltd (a company registered in the United Kingdom, collectively referred to as "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application, Iron Hegemony (the "App").

This Privacy Policy may be provided in multiple languages. In the event of any discrepancy or conflict among the translations, the English version shall prevail.

1. Territorial Scope

The App is not intended for and is not authorized for distribution, download, or use in Mainland China, Taiwan, or India. We do not target or direct the App to users in these territories. If you are located in these territories, please do not download or use the App.

2. Information Collection and Use

The App is designed as a strictly offline, standalone experience. We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal identifiable information (PII). We do not require account registration, and the App is not integrated with any telemetry, background analytics SDKs, or usage tracking mechanisms that communicate with our or third-party servers.

Non-Personal Data: The App only processes non-identifiable gameplay data locally on your device, including game progress, campaign saves, and configuration settings. This data remains entirely on your device, and we have no access to it.

3. Local Data and System Backups

All game data is stored exclusively in your device's local storage. We have no access to this data. Please note that your device's operating system (e.g., Apple iOS) may automatically back up app data to your personal cloud storage (e.g., iCloud). This is a system-level function governed entirely by the respective device provider’s privacy policy; we have no control over it and assume no liability for such backups.

4. Third-Party Services and External Links

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising networks, data analytics, or social media SDKs. The App may contain static text links in the "About" page to third-party data source providers (e.g., Natural Earth). Clicking these links will invoke your device's native browser. We do not track this outbound behavior and are not responsible for the privacy practices, cookies, or content of external websites.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is a geopolitical and tactical war simulation game intended only for mature or adolescent audiences. We have no intention of soliciting or collecting data from any minors. Given the App's strictly offline and zero-data collection nature, its design does not pose risks of violating the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) regarding children's data protection. Minors should only use the App under the guidance of a parent or legal guardian. If parents or guardians have any concerns about local data on the device, they can clear the app data directly through the device's system settings.

6. Your Rights, Data Deletion, and Complaint Mechanism

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, you may autonomously and completely exercise control over your data by uninstalling the App from your device. Uninstalling the App will result in all game-related data stored locally on your device being permanently cleared. For any system backup files that your operating system may have generated and stored in third-party cloud services (e.g., iCloud), you must separately manage or delete them through your account settings. We have no access to or control over such backups.

In accordance with the UK Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (DUAA) and related data protection laws, if you have any complaints or concerns regarding our privacy practices, you have the right to raise them directly with us. Please contact us at support@3tstone.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days of receipt and investigate and respond without undue delay. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at https://ico.org.uk/.

7. Map Data and Content Disclaimer

Map boundaries, country names, and related data in this game (partially based on Natural Earth data) are for entertainment and simulation purposes only and do not represent 3TStone Ltd's official endorsement or stance on any real-world territorial sovereignty, political boundaries, or diplomatic positions. The game contains partially fictional data and should not be used as a real geographical, political, or legal reference.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy, and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

9. Contact Information

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at: support@3tstone.com. Any personal information you provide when contacting us (such as your email address) will be used solely to respond to your inquiry and will not be retained beyond what is reasonably necessary for that purpose.

10. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. The updated policy will be posted within the App and will become effective immediately upon posting.
