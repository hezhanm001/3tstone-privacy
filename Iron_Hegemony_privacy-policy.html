This Privacy Policy explains how 3TStone (a company registered in the United Kingdom, collectively referred to as "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application, Iron Hegemony (the "App").

1. Territorial Scope

The App is not intended for distribution or use in Mainland China, Taiwan, or India. Users in these territories are not authorized to use the App. 3TStone does not target or direct the App to users in these territories.

2. Information Collection and Use

The App is designed as a standalone, strictly offline experience. We do not intentionally collect, store, transmit, or process any personal identifiable information (PII). We do not require account registration, and the App contains no telemetry, background analytics SDKs, or usage tracking mechanisms that communicate with our servers.

3. Local Data and System Backups

All gameplay data, progression, campaign saves, and configuration profiles are stored locally and exclusively on the internal storage of your device. We have no logical or physical access to this data.

System-Level Backups: Depending on your device settings, your operating system (e.g., Apple iOS) may automatically back up the App’s local data to your personal cloud storage (e.g., iCloud). This backup process is a system-level function governed entirely by your device provider’s privacy policy. 3TStone has no access to, control over, or liability for such cloud backups.

4. Third-Party Services and External Links

The App does not integrate third-party advertising networks or social media data-sharing modules.

The App may contain attribution links in its "About" section to third-party data providers (e.g., Natural Earth). Tapping these links will invoke your device's native system mechanism to open an external web browser. We do not track these outbound clicks, and we are not responsible for the privacy practices, cookies, or content of external websites.

5. Children's Privacy

The App features geopolitical and tactical war simulation themes appropriate for a mature or adolescent audience. We do not knowingly solicit or collect any data from minors. By virtue of operating fully offline with zero data collection, the App natively complies with international child privacy frameworks (such as COPPA and UK GDPR). Minors should only use the App under the appropriate guidance of a parent or legal guardian.

6. User Rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, any requests regarding data access, correction, or deletion can be fulfilled completely and autonomously by you. To delete all data associated with the App, simply uninstall the App from your device.

7. Contact Information

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at: support@3tstone.com. Any personal information you provide when contacting us (such as your email address) will be used solely to respond to your inquiry and will not be retained beyond what is necessary for that purpose.
