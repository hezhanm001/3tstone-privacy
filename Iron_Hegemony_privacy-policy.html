Iron Hegemony (the “App”) is a strategy warfare game provided by 3TStone Ltd (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal information when you use the App and related services.

We provide this Policy in multiple languages. If there is any inconsistency, the English version prevails unless applicable law requires otherwise.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account details such as email address, sign-in identifier, nickname, avatar, and region.
  • Player support messages, bug reports, survey responses, and other communications you send to us.
  • Information related to purchases, refund requests, and account recovery.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical data, such as device model, operating system, language, time zone, app version, IP address, and identifiers needed for security or analytics.
  • Gameplay data, such as progress, missions, alliance participation, leaderboard activity, battle records, and event participation.
  • Log and diagnostic data used to maintain service stability, prevent cheating, and investigate abuse.

Optional features

  • If you enable notifications, we process push token data to send gameplay, account, or event notices.
  • If you use chat, alliance, or social features, we process the content and related metadata needed to operate and moderate those features.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App and game services.
  • Create and manage accounts, authenticate users, and save game progress.
  • Operate multiplayer, alliance, ranking, event, and customer support functions.
  • Protect the App, detect fraud, cheating, exploitation, harassment, and other rule violations.
  • Process purchases, restore entitlements, and handle refunds or disputes where applicable.
  • Analyze usage trends, fix bugs, and improve performance and balance.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Legal Bases

Where required by applicable law, we process personal information based on one or more of the following legal bases: performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the App, such as cloud hosting, analytics, authentication, payment processing, customer support, moderation, and security providers.

We may also disclose information when necessary to comply with law, protect users, enforce our rights, investigate abuse, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset transfer.

5. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards, such as contractual protections, access controls, and other lawful transfer mechanisms.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain player accounts, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, comply with law, and protect the security and integrity of the service.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of certain personal information. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

8. Children and Age Requirements

Iron Hegemony is not directed to children below the minimum age permitted under applicable law. Where required, minors must have consent from a parent or guardian before using the App or making purchases.

9. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Contact

If you have questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, contact 3TStone Ltd at support@3tstone.com.
