1. Information we handle

Account and sign-in information

When cloud account features are available and you create or sign in to an account, we may process your email address, Firebase user ID, display name, account creation and update information, and authentication information needed for sign-in, password reset and account security. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored in the Patinote diary database as plain text.

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple and Firebase may provide an account identifier, authorization credential, and, where Apple makes it available, a name or private relay email address.

Location and visit-footprint information

If you allow location access while using the app, Patinote may process a one-time foreground location result. This can include latitude, longitude, time, horizontal accuracy, country, administrative area, city, time zone and a city identifier derived from that information. Apple’s reverse-geocoding service may receive location data to determine a place name.

If you choose to enable an automatic visit-footprint feature in a cloud-enabled version, Patinote may attempt to create a separate visit record for the derived city and local calendar day when you actively use the app and location is successfully recognized. This is not continuous background tracking. Turning the feature or iOS location permission off stops future location attempts but does not itself erase records already stored.

Location can be inaccurate because of signal conditions, device settings, surrounding conditions, map or geocoding data, and administrative-boundary data. Patinote is not for emergency services, navigation, or legal proof of location.

Journal content and media

You may provide journal text, moods, date and time, a venue name, country, administrative area, city, time zone, and location information connected with an entry. You may also choose to attach up to six photos and one short video. Media may contain people, voices, location metadata or other information that you choose to upload.

Photos may be resized and converted to JPEG, and videos may be checked and converted to MP4 to support the service. We do not guarantee that EXIF, GPS, embedded location, people or other metadata are removed from media. Do not assume uploaded media are anonymous.

Device data and local storage

The app may store language preferences, age self-confirmation, legal-document status, downloaded administrative-boundary packs and media display caches on your device. Cloud database software may also maintain an offline device cache. Cached or temporary files may remain until the app, you or the operating system clears them; they are not a backup service.

2. How and why we use information

We use information to provide and protect Patinote, including account access, journal and footprint features, maps and trips, media display and upload, support, abuse prevention, service reliability and legal compliance. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of our contract with you; your consent for optional processing where required; our legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention and reliable service; and compliance with legal obligations.

We do not use journal content, media or precise location for cross-app or cross-site behavioural advertising, and we do not sell or rent those data. If we introduce advertising, tracking, public sharing, AI training, live community statistics or another materially different purpose, we will update this Policy and provide notices or choices required by law before using data for that purpose.

3. Cloud services and recipients

Where cloud features are configured and you sign in, the deployed version of Patinote may use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage for Firebase and Cloud Functions for Firebase to process account, journal, media, visit-footprint, deletion and legal-acceptance requests. We may also use Apple services such as Core Location, CLGeocoder, PhotosPicker and Sign in with Apple.

If you request an offline administrative-boundary download, geoBoundaries and an approved download host may receive the selected country and administrative level together with normal network information such as IP address and request headers. The app should identify any additional map, WebView, geocoding or boundary-data providers actually used in the released version before publication.

Service providers may process data only as needed to provide services to us, comply with law, protect rights and safety, or follow our documented instructions. We may also disclose information when required by law, to address fraud or security concerns, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or asset transaction, subject to applicable notice requirements.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, a copy, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent and, where applicable, information about automated processing. You can manage iOS location permission, remove offline boundary packs and use available in-app deletion tools. You can contact us at support@3tstone.com to make another request. We may request reasonable verification to protect your account.

California residents may have rights to know, access, correct, delete and limit certain uses of sensitive personal information, including precise geolocation, and to opt out of a sale or sharing where those concepts apply. Before publication, this Policy must state the actual sale/sharing position and provide any legally required request mechanisms.

7. Security

We use measures appropriate to the nature of the information, such as authentication, access controls and managed cloud services. No system is perfectly secure. Patinote does not promise end-to-end encryption or absolute confidentiality. Please protect your device, password and Apple ID and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

8. Children and minimum age
